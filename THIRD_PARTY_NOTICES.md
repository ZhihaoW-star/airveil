# Materials and dependencies

- The app uses Apple system frameworks supplied by macOS. They are linked at
  runtime, not bundled as copied third-party libraries.
- Interface symbols come from macOS system symbol APIs, not bundled symbol files.
- The release icon and simulated desktop are original procedural drawings.
  The anonymous character illustration was generated with OpenAI image generation.
  No real screens, identifiable people, device names, or private documents appear.
  See `tools/media/README.md` for provenance and reproduction instructions.
- The media generator uses Pillow and NumPy as optional development tools. They are
  not bundled in the application; their respective licenses apply to those tools.
- Fonts are rendered using locally installed fonts; no font files are shipped.

Apple, AirPods, and macOS names identify compatibility. AirVeil does not claim
endorsement or affiliation.
